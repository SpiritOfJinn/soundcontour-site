Sound Contour

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Sound Contour is a system-wide audio equalizer for macOS, with an optional iPhone companion app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we do not collect, sell, or share any of your data.

Data we collect

None. Sound Contour has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

Free trial

Sound Contour does not require an account. To know when your 7-day free trial ends, the app saves the date your trial started on your device (in the app's settings and your device's Keychain). This date stays on your device.

Purchases

The one-time Sound Contour Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment information.

Your settings and presets

Your equalizer settings and presets are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they may sync between your own devices through your private iCloud account, which Apple operates. We cannot access your iCloud data.

Audio

Sound Contour processes your Mac's audio in real time on your device so it can apply the equalizer. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

iPhone companion app

The companion app talks directly to Sound Contour on your Mac over your local network. That connection stays on your network and does not go through any server.

Children

Sound Contour does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
